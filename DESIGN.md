# Kenjineer Games Studio / Brand identity

## Scope

Studio identity for games across genres and platforms. The identity must not depend on Nova, a palace, mobile devices, or the size of the development team. This change covers the header mark and a reusable brand foundation; approved website copy remains intact.

## Direction

A compact, playful geometric K. Three interlocking game-piece forms make one clear silhouette. Broad strokes, deliberate open notches and gently softened corners make it legible at small sizes. No enclosing circle, KGS badge, controller, crown, sparkle, glow, or faux 3D treatment.

The mark accompanies the full studio name. The name remains selectable HTML text with an accessible home-link label. The mark is decorative within that link.

## Generation brief

Create one original flat geometric K symbol for Kenjineer Games Studio. Fuse two interlocking game pieces into a compact readable K, with one generous negative-space notch, broad strokes, gently softened outer corners, no enclosing shape and no fine lines. Friendly, confident, contemporary game publisher identity, independent of game genre or platform. Flat solid blue, no gradient, no shadows, no texture, no wordmark, no letters other than the K-shaped symbol. Center the single mark with restrained safe space. First master on white; production version on genuine transparency for the navy header.

Production edit: preserve the generated three-piece K construction; change the fill to pale sky blue #94C9FA and use genuine alpha transparency in the background, holes and gaps. No shadow, gradient, texture, matte or outline. Prepare for 40px display on navy.
