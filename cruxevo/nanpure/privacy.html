Privacy Policy

Cruxevo: Sudoku · Version 1 · October 4, 2026

Kenjineer Games Studio ("we") develops Cruxevo: Sudoku ("the App"). This policy explains information handled by the App and by our support service.

1. Game data and accounts

The App stores game progress, entries and notes, statistics, settings, coins, and upgrades on your device. We do not operate an account system, a game-data server, or a cloud-save service. The game can be played offline; advertising needs an internet connection.

Your operating system may back up or transfer app data depending on the device and its settings. Such operating-system features are separate from a developer-operated cloud-save service. We cannot promise that data always remains on a single device.

2. Advertising and information sent to Google

The App uses Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK), provided by Google LLC, for rewarded and interstitial ads. The SDK can collect and share the following information from your device for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate your approximate location.
  • App and ad interactions, such as launches, taps, and video views.
  • Diagnostic information about the app and SDK, such as launch time, hangs, and energy usage.
  • Device identifiers, including the app set ID where used by the SDK.

The current Android manifest removes permissions for the Android advertising ID and Android advertising-services identifiers, attribution, and topics. This does not disable all SDK data transmission or all other identifiers. The App does not request precise device location.

Google states that data collected by its Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit using TLS. Google may process information outside your country, including in the United States. Its retention and handling are governed by its policies; we do not promise a fixed retention period or automatic deletion within 90 days for data held by Google.

Read Google's Privacy Policy, Google Mobile Ads data disclosures, and Google's ad privacy controls. Device or provider controls may affect particular identifiers; they do not necessarily stop all advertising communication.

3. Children and age information

The App is intended for children and adults. All users receive the same child-directed advertising treatment and maximum G ad-content rating. We do not use an entered birth date to change ad treatment, and the App does not ask for a birth date.

Child-directed and G settings are advertising safeguards; they do not mean that no information is transmitted. We do not authorize behavioral targeting for users of this App. Children and parents may contact us about information handling or request review of information supplied to support.

The current implementation does not use Play Age Signals. It does not receive or store age information through that API.

4. Support messages

If you email us, we receive your email address, message, and attachments you choose to send. We use them to respond, investigate reported problems, and handle privacy inquiries. Do not send passwords, identity documents, birth dates, or unnecessary information about children. We may ask for limited additional information needed to understand a request.

Support correspondence is handled using our email provider and may be stored outside your country. We keep it only as needed to resolve and document the inquiry, handle related disputes, or meet legal obligations, then delete it when it is no longer needed. We do not use support messages for advertising.

5. Retention, deletion, and your choices

Local game data remains until it is removed through Android's app-storage controls or the App is uninstalled. Backups or device-transfer copies may remain under your operating system's controls and may be restored. Uninstalling the App stops further App-initiated advertising requests from that installation; it does not delete information Google already holds.

We cannot recover game data that we do not hold, or directly delete Google's independently controlled records. For Google-held data, use Google's privacy controls and contact channels. The current App has no in-app data-deletion request form or privacy-consent settings screen.

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing of your personal information, withdraw consent where processing relies on it, obtain a portable copy, or complain to your local data-protection authority. Contact us about information we hold and questions about the App. We will assess requests under applicable law; we may need to verify that the information concerns you or a child you are authorized to represent.

6. Changes and contact

We will update this policy when information handling changes and identify the revision date. Where applicable law requires additional notice or consent, a policy update alone does not replace it.

1. ゲームデータとアカウント

進捗、入力数字・メモ、統計、設定、コイン、強化は端末に保存します。運営者はアカウント、ゲームデータ用の独自サーバー、クラウドセーブサービスを提供していません。ゲームはオフラインで遊べますが、広告には通信が必要です。

2. 広告とGoogleへの外部送信

リワード広告とインタースティシャル広告に、Google LLCのGoogle AdMob（Google Mobile Ads SDK）を利用します。SDKは広告配信、分析、不正行為防止のために、端末から次の情報を収集・共有する場合があります。

  • IPアドレス：おおよその現在地の推定に使われる場合があります。
  • アプリや広告の操作情報：起動、タップ、動画視聴など。
  • アプリ・SDKの診断情報：起動時間、動作停止、消費電力など。
  • 端末識別子：SDKが利用する場合のアプリセットIDなど。

現行Android設定では、Android広告IDと広告サービスの識別子・アトリビューション・トピックに関する権限を削除しています。この措置でSDKのすべての通信や他の識別子が無効になるわけではありません。本アプリは端末の正確な位置情報を要求しません。

Googleの説明では、Mobile Ads SDKが収集する情報はTLSで暗号化して送信されます。米国を含む居住国外で処理される場合があります。Googleが保有する情報の保存期間や取り扱いは同社の方針に従い、運営者は一律の保存期間や90日以内の自動削除を約束しません。

Googleのプライバシーポリシー、広告SDKのデータ開示、Googleの広告プライバシー設定をご確認ください。端末や事業者の設定で一部の識別子を制御できる場合がありますが、広告通信すべてが止まるとは限りません。
